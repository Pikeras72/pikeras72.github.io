    Can a neural network learn to draw in Lego?

    03

    2025 · EQUITIA · Bachelor's thesis

    Does an AI answer differently if you change a single word?

    My bachelor's thesis, done with Telefónica in their TUTORÍA programme. It's a tool that asks a language model hundreds of questions, changing only who they're about (their age, their origin…), and measures whether the answers change with it. Six kinds of test, from closed questions to prompt injection attempts, and a report that flags anything out of the ordinary. I got a 10 out of 10.

    What stuck with me: measuring well is harder than building, and an AI can sound neutral without being neutral.

    • Python
    • LLMs
    • Hugging Face
    • Statistics

    04

    2026 · Three Walls · MSc in Applied AI (UC3M)

    Can agents that don't trust each other negotiate?

    Three factions, Coloso, Enjambre and Errante, separated by walls. All three builders need stone at the same time; if their quartermaster has none, it leans out of the window in the wall and bargains with another faction: bread for stone. BDI agents (beliefs, desires and intentions) that talk to each other with FIPA protocols, built in GAMA.

    What stuck with me: in a system with many agents, the hard part isn't each agent but what happens between them.

    • GAMA · GAML
    • BDI agents
    • FIPA protocols
